## 🧠 How I approach systems

- I focus on behavior, not just vulnerabilities.
- I care more about fundamentals than frameworks.
- I document assumptions, failures, and edge cases.
- I prefer simple techniques that scale mentally.

Some repositories here are exploratory by design. That's intentional.
I'd rather publish material that is useful, reproducible, and safe to share than polished but empty showcases.
